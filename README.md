# Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of end-to-end data analysis projects showcasing skills in Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, and Generative AI — built to demonstrate both Data Analyst and Business Analyst capabilities.

## Projects

### 📊 [HR Employee Attrition Analysis](./hr-attrition-analysis)
Analysis of employee attrition patterns across departments and roles using the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset, including a Power BI dashboard and a Generative AI-powered natural language Q&A feature over the dataset.
Tools: Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, Generative AI (Gemini)
Key finding: Overall attrition rate of 16.12%, with Sales Representatives showing the highest attrition at 39.8%.

### 🍔 [Swiggy vs Zomato Platform Performance Analysis](./swiggy-vs-zomato-comparison)
Comparative analysis of 8,000 restaurant-platform records across 10 Indian cities, evaluating Swiggy and Zomato on revenue, order volume, ratings, and delivery performance.
Tools: Python, SQL (MySQL), Power BI, Excel
Key finding: Platform performance nearly balanced overall (Zomato better in 46.8% of cases vs. Swiggy 46.4%), with revenue leadership split 5 cities to 5.

### 📉 [Customer Churn & Revenue Risk Analytics](./customer-churn-analysis)
End-to-end churn and revenue-risk analysis across 7,032 customer records, identifying high-risk segments and revenue at risk to support targeted retention campaigns.
Tools: MySQL, Power BI, Advanced DAX, Excel
Key finding: 26.58% overall churn rate, with $139.13K in Monthly Recurring Revenue at risk, concentrated among early-tenure, month-to-month customers.
