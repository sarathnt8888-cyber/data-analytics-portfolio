# 📊 Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of end-to-end data analysis projects showcasing skills in Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, DAX, and Generative AI — built to demonstrate both Data Analyst and Business Analyst capabilities.

---

## 🚀 Projects

### 📊 1. [HR Employee Attrition Analysis](./hr-attrition-analysis)

Analysis of employee attrition patterns across departments and roles using the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset, including a Power BI dashboard and a Generative AI-powered natural language Q&A feature over the dataset.

Tools: Excel · Python · SQL · Power BI · Generative AI (Gemini)

Key Finding: Overall attrition rate was 16.12%, with Sales Representatives showing the highest attrition at 39.8%.

➡️ [View Project](./hr-attrition-analysis)

---

### 🍔 2. [Swiggy vs Zomato Platform Performance Analysis](./swiggy-vs-zomato-comparison)

Comparative analysis of 8,000 restaurant-platform records across 10 Indian cities, evaluating Swiggy and Zomato based on revenue, order volume, ratings, and delivery performance.

Tools: Python · SQL (MySQL) · Power BI · Excel

Key Finding: Overall platform performance was nearly balanced, with Zomato leading in 46.8% of comparisons vs. Swiggy at 46.4%, while revenue leadership was split evenly across the 10 cities.

➡️ [View Project](./swiggy-vs-zomato-comparison)

---

### 📉 3. [Customer Churn & Revenue Risk Analytics](./customer-churn-analysis)

End-to-end churn and revenue-risk analysis across 7,032 customer records, identifying high-risk customer segments and revenue at risk to support targeted retention strategies.

Tools: MySQL · Power BI · DAX · Excel

Key Finding: Overall churn was 26.58%, with $139.13K in Monthly Recurring Revenue at risk, particularly among early-tenure and month-to-month customers.

➡️ [View Project](./customer-churn-analysis)

---

### 🛒 4. [Online Shopping UX & Conversion Analysis](./online-shopping-ux-analysis)

Analyzed 12,205 online shopping sessions to understand purchase conversion, visitor behavior, engagement patterns, and potential UX opportunities.

Tools: Python · pandas · SQL · Power BI · DAX

Key Finding: Overall conversion was 15.63%, with an approximately 10.8 percentage-point conversion gap between new and returning visitors. The analysis supported a proposed recently viewed products UX test for returning visitors.

➡️ [View Project](./online-shopping-ux-analysis)

---

### 💰 5. [Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis](./Retail_sales_profitability_analysis/)

Analyzed retail sales data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, regional trends, category performance, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

Tools: Excel · MySQL · Power BI · DAX

Key Finding: The business generated approximately $2.3M in sales and $286.4K in profit with a 12.47% profit margin. Furniture generated substantial sales but comparatively weak profitability, while higher discount levels were generally associated with lower profitability.

➡️ [View Project](./Retail_sales_profitability_analysis/)
